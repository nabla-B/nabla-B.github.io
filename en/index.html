What we do

A device is only finished when it is certified, boots, and delivers data in the field. We cover the whole path — or exactly the segment you are missing.

Embedded hardware

Architecture, schematic and layout in KiCad, bring-up, EMC preparation and certification, handover to manufacturing — including supply-chain experience with PCB and cable manufacturers in China.

Firmware & bring-up

Software bring-up and firmware for microcontrollers (bare-metal, RTOS), FPGAs (VHDL/Verilog) and Zynq SoCs — up to a Yocto Linux with documented SBOM and update path.

Decentralised measurement & DAQ

Measurement intelligence where the data originates: analog front-ends, sensors, PoE networks, time base, telemetry and monitoring infrastructure.

Consulting & training

Feasibility, architecture reviews, toolchain rescue. Training for engineers in digital measurement technology and data acquisition — on site or online.
