skAInet Edge-Compute

2016 – 2026 · Auto-Intern GmbH / skAInet

Programmable M12-PoE switch, router and Linux compute node for industrial edge data acquisition: 7 sealed PoE ports, 8-core ARM, Yocto Linux with documented SBOM. System architecture, PoE-first concept, M12 cabling specification and EMC certification.
